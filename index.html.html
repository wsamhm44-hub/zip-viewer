Choose a .zip or database file

A .zip library, or the .db / .sqlite files on their own — select several at once and they open as one library. Picking the databases directly skips unpacking, which matters when one of them is several gigabytes.

Reading…
Everything stays on your device. There is no server and no upload.
Big files are fine. Only the item you open is decompressed, so a 10 GB zip won't exhaust memory.
Your library is remembered. Names, folders and progress are kept, so reopening is instant.
.rar won't open here. Convert it to .zip first — on iPad the free Documents app can do this.
